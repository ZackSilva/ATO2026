## Research limits

Public SportsCardsPro tables expose the latest 30 entries per grade bucket; popular issues often exhaust that list within weeks or months. Public PSA certificate pages generally show recent similar sales. Targeted PSA indexed auction tables recovered substantial Jordan and Barkley history. The football research did not establish dense two-year coverage: Brady has no qualifying observations before August 2026, and Sanders has a September 2025 cluster followed by September–October 2026. Barry's [CardFacts page](https://www.psacard.com/cardfacts/football-cards/1989-score/barry-sanders-257/13033) exceeded the retrieval size limit, direct public access returned HTTP 403, and full auction histories redirected to sign-in. Searches returning Deion Sanders, Pro Set Barry Sanders, Bowman Chrome, other years, or other grades were excluded. Some primary auction lots do establish older actual dates, but report prices including buyer premiums; those totals are excluded from the item-price import pack. No premium was guessed or reverse-calculated from today's fee schedule.

This is a selected public research artifact, not a live feed or an appraisal. A fuller historical refresh needs dated exact-grade item-price transactions with individual listing references from an accessible archive or operator-provided export. A source's proprietary monthly price curve is not a set of individual completed sales and cannot be substituted for those observations.
